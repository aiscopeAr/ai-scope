Executive Summary

The channel is factually reliable and technically stable — messages send, links resolve, HTML escaping is airtight. But it reads as one long news ticker, not a premium editorial brand.

The core finding: 93 of the last 100 posts were sent before the Sprint 2/3 template system existed — they use the old single-paragraph, asterisk-Markdown format the codebase's own commit history shows was already flagged as broken (parse_mode mismatch, fixed 2026‑07‑24). Only the 7 most recent posts (this morning) reflect the new "[Template] Headline → paragraph → highlights → CTA → hashtags" structure. So the channel's actual, lived reader experience for the past month is the old format — and this audit treats that as the real baseline.

Within that baseline, two patterns dominate everything else: 94% of headlines open with just two words — "هل" (does/is) or "كيف" (how) — turning the channel feed into a wall of interchangeable rhetorical questions; and 91% of captions are hard-truncated mid-sentence at 200 characters with no ellipsis or completion, which reads as broken, not concise. The new template (visible in the last 7 posts) already fixes the truncation problem and adds structure — it just hasn't been backfilled or promoted into the reader's actual day-to-day experience yet, and headline diversity wasn't part of that fix.

Current Weaknesses

Ranked by how much of the 100-post sample each defect touches.

High · 94/100

Headline monotony

94% of headlines start with "هل" (53) or "كيف" (35) — the AI prompt's own instruction to "pose the deeper question" (lib/review-openai.ts:68) is being followed too literally, at scale. The channel feed reads as the same rhetorical trick repeated 94 times in a row.

2 distinct opening words across 94 posts
High · 91/100

Mid-sentence truncation

The legacy caption builder hard-cuts at 200 characters with no sentence-boundary check and no ellipsis — captions stop on a dangling preposition or half a clause. The new truncateSummary() already fixes this for future posts.

200 char hard cutoff, no "…"
High · 93/100

Flat single-paragraph body

No template label, no highlights, no reading time, no distinct CTA line — just an emoji, a bold title, one dense paragraph, and a bare link. Nothing signals content type before the reader taps.

1 visual "shape" for every post type
Medium · 3/6

Template coverage gap

Of 6 defined templates (breaking / guide / comparison / tool / research / general), only 3 have ever fired in production data: أداة (tool), عاجل (breaking), دليل (guide). Comparison and research paths are unproven against real content.

3 of 6 templates ever seen live
Medium · 6/100

Hashtag thinness (legacy)

Old-format posts carry a static, near-identical pair — #ذكاء_اصطناعي #AI — repeated regardless of topic. The new deterministic engine (lib/social/hashtags.ts) is a real fix, but it's only live on the 7 newest posts.

2 generic tags reused ~94 times
Medium · 2/100

Broken links

2 posts link to /reviews/ with an empty slug — a dead 404 sent to the whole channel. Root cause is upstream of Telegram formatting (a null/empty slug reaching approveReview), outside this sprint's scope, but worth flagging.

2 dead article links sent live
Medium · 10/100

Split-domain links

10 legacy posts still link to ai-news-ar.vercel.app instead of lumiq.news — a rebrand artifact. Cosmetic, but a premium channel shouldn't visibly show its own migration history to readers.

2 domains live in one feed
Medium

No image in the send

telegram.ts calls sendMessage, never sendPhoto — the OG image only appears via Telegram's own link-preview crawler, which is smaller, sometimes delayed, and can be suppressed by Telegram entirely. The channel's visual identity is currently at the mercy of a preview, not a deliberate send.

02

Premium Telegram Editorial Style Guide

One identity per content type. Icons are load-bearing — each earns its place by making the post instantly sortable in a crowded feed, not decorating it.

TypeIconSignalsVoice
🚨 عاجل — Breaking🚨 Same-day publish. Time-sensitive, first-to-report framing. Direct, urgent, no rhetorical question — state what happened.
📘 دليل — Guide📘 Tutorials category, "كيف/دليل/شرح" titles. How-to and explainer pieces. Instructive, second person, promises a concrete takeaway.
⚖️ مقارنة — Comparison⚖️ [[compare:]] content token. Head-to-head framing. Neutral, names both sides explicitly in the headline.
🛠 أداة — AI Tool🛠 [[tool:]] token or ai-tools category. Product-first. Practical — what it does, who it's for, not hype adjectives.
📊 تحليل وأبحاث — Research📊 Research category. Papers, benchmarks, findings. Precise, cites the source institution/lab by name in-line.
🏢 الشركات — Company🏢 New — currently folds into "general." Funding, partnerships, leadership, strategy. Names the company and the stake first, not the implication.
🎯 Prompt / Playbook🎯 New — for the Prompts Library feature once it feeds Telegram. Actionable, single clear payoff stated up front.

Rule across all seven: exactly one emoji per message, placed only in the template tag — never sprinkled through the body. The current system already does this correctly; keep it that way as templates are added.

03

Headline Improvements

The fix isn't "stop asking questions" — a well-placed question works. It's forcing variety in how the deeper angle gets phrased, so the feed doesn't read as one template repeated 94 times.

Retire (overused)

  • هل يمكن لـ… أن …؟ ×31
  • كيف تعيد … تشكيل …؟ ×14

Prefer: declarative stance

  • Gemini 3.5 يتفوق على النماذج الصينية — لكن بثمن
  • OpenAI تراهن على ChatGPT كأداة عمل، لا محادثة

Prefer: named tension

  • Microsoft وMistral: شراكة أم استحواذ ناعم؟
  • الأدوات اللاكودية تتوسع أسرع من الرقابة عليها

Prefer: number/stakes-led

  • 3 مختبرات، سباق واحد: من يملك المستقبل الفيزيائي للذكاء؟
  • جامعة براون تكتشف الغش — والذكاء الاصطناعي في قفص الاتهام

Suggested guardrail for the drafting prompt: cap "هل"/"كيف" openers at roughly 1 in 3 posts, and give the model 3–4 named alternative headline shapes (declarative stance, named tension, stakes-led, number-led) to rotate through — the same discipline already used for CTA selection below.

04

CTA Library

The template system already assigns one CTA per type — good instinct, but each type currently has exactly one phrase, so it repeats every time that template fires. Expanding each to a rotating pool of 3 keeps the "right CTA for the type" logic while killing the repetition.

Selection can stay fully deterministic — no LLM needed — by rotating on reviewId hash modulo pool size, the same "derived from fields that already exist" principle telegram-templates.ts already uses for template classification.

05

Hashtag Recommendations

lib/social/hashtags.ts is already the right design — deterministic, tag-first, capped at 4, with a generic-category denylist. It just isn't reaching most posts yet because most posts predate it.

Drop

Static pair spam

#ذكاء_اصطناعي #AI appended to nearly every legacy post regardless of topic — appeared 6× even in the small sample checked, and effectively 100× across the full legacy set. Superseded by the new engine; no further action needed beyond backfill (Section 09).

Keep

Product/company/tech tags

New-format posts already surface exactly what the sprint brief asks for: #GPT_56 #GPT_Red #OpenAI, #Hugging_Face — specific, article-level, non-generic. This is the target state; just needs volume.

Watch

Category-name hashtags read soft

Tags like #التصنيف-derived category names (e.g. a literal category label as a hashtag) are weaker than product/tech tags — they're accurate but not something a reader would search. Keep them as tag #3–4, never #1.

Title sizing
Keep the 2-tier rule (38px >60 chars, 46px under) — it works. Add a 3rd tier at 32px for titles >80 chars instead of hard-truncating at 80.
Category pill
Good as-is — indigo pill reads clearly at thumbnail size. Verify contrast holds when background photo is very light (rare but possible).
Background photo opacity
0.2 full-bleed + 0.55 right-strip is correct — keeps text legible over any source photo without needing per-image adjustment.
Mobile crop risk
Telegram's own preview often shows only the left ~60% width — keep title and brand mark inside that zone; never rely on the right image strip alone for identity.
Reading-time chip
Not present. Add a small chip next to the category pill — reuses data already computed in telegram-message.ts's estimateReadingMinutes(), no new field needed.
Summary line
120-char slice can also cut mid-word like captions do. Reuse truncateSummary()'s sentence-boundary logic here instead of a raw .slice().

Recommended Final Refinements

Ordered by leverage — what closes the gap between the audited baseline and the channel this sprint's own template system already proves is possible.

P0

Backfill is the highest-leverage fix available

The new template system already solves headline structure, truncation, and hashtag quality — it's just live on 7 of 100 posts. Nothing in this audit needs "inventing"; it needs the existing Sprint 2/3 system reaching the other 93%. (Presentation-only — re-sending isn't in scope here, but regenerating stored captions for reference/QA is.)

P0

Add headline-shape rotation to the drafting prompt

One line change to lib/review-openai.ts's title instruction: replace "pose the deeper question" with a rotating set of 3–4 named shapes (declarative-stance, named-tension, stakes-led, question) so "هل/كيف" stops being the only pattern the model reaches for.

P1

Fix the 2 broken article links

Trace why slug reached approveReview empty for one review — likely an editor-side validation gap, not a Telegram-layer bug. Outside this sprint's presentation scope but worth a ticket.

P1

Expand the CTA and hashtag pools from 1→3 per template

Both engines are already deterministic and correctly typed — this is additive data, not new logic. See Sections 04–05 for the specific phrases.

P1

Loosen comparison/research template triggers

classifyReviewTemplate() requires an explicit [[compare:]] token for the ⚖️ template — Example 05 shows a genuine two-model comparison that never got tagged with one. Consider also matching on "مقابل" / "مقارنة بـ" in the title as a secondary signal.

P1

Send the OG image directly, not just via link preview

Switching the provider call from sendMessage to sendPhoto (caption as the photo caption, same escaped HTML) guarantees the premium visual actually reaches every reader instead of depending on Telegram's own preview crawler. This does touch the provider file, so treat it as a distinct, reviewable follow-up rather than bundling it into this presentation-only sprint.

P1

Normalize legacy links to lumiq.news

10 posts still point at ai-news-ar.vercel.app. A redirect at the domain level (if not already in place) neutralizes this without touching any Telegram code at all.

P1

Add reading-time and a tighter summary to the OG image

Reuses estimateReadingMinutes() and truncateSummary(), both of which already exist for Telegram — pure reuse, no new computation.
