5. Weak titles

134 of 236 reviews (57%) use a generic rhetorical-question title with no named entity

Priority 1

Pattern: titles open with "هل يمكن…" / "كيف ت…" / "ما وراء…" and never name a specific model, company, or product. Examples: "هل يمكن للإنسانية السيطرة على الذكاء الاصطناعي قبل فوات الأوان؟", "كيف يُعزّز الذكاء الاصطناعي النفوذ الدولي؟". These don't match real search query patterns — people search "GPT-5 مميزات" or "Claude مقابل Gemini," not the literal rhetorical question. By contrast, the "what is X" guides with named entities (Gemini, Claude, Midjourney) are the corpus's actual traffic drivers.

Regex match: title starts with generic opener AND contains no recognized proper noun, across all 236 titles

6. Weak meta descriptions

37 of 51 tools (73%) have SEO descriptions under 100 characters, mostly templated

Priority 1

Shared pattern: "اكتشف [Tool]... جربها الآن!" ("Discover [Tool]... try it now!") — generic, no differentiator, no pricing signal, no comparison keyword. P3

Priority 3 — Long-term

Real opportunities, but either dependent on traffic/authority the site doesn't have yet, or requiring net-new strategy rather than editing existing content.

  • Populate the 6 empty categories with genuinely new writing — confirmed (§Categories) this can't be shortcut via recategorization; it's a content-creation project, not an editing one.
  • Systematic in-body linking as a permanent editorial rule, not a one-time cleanup pass — the 1.7% baseline should become a checklist item in the publish pipeline itself.
  • Full retitle pass across all 134 generic-titled reviews — P1/P2 cover the highest-view ~50; the long tail needs the same treatment eventually.
  • Comparison coverage for all remaining categories — once the 7 highest-value pairs (P1) ship, extend to lower-traffic categories as they grow.
  • Revisit the 428 true-singleton tags — most will never justify a hub page individually, but periodically check if any cluster has grown past the 3-article threshold.
  • Author-level voice audit — the generic-title pattern correlates with specific system-prompt phrasing (zayd/lina's rhetorical-question openers); a prompt-level fix would prevent future titles from needing this same retroactive cleanup.
  • Prompt category rebalancing — "general" at 55% works today, but monitor whether it becomes a dumping ground as volume grows past 272.
